# Seed City demo UX

Upstream habits: better-writing (plain words), impeccable Operate + distill/clarify.

## Rules

1. **One job per rail section.** Headline = what you do. Supporting line = what you will see.
2. **No jargon in the first screenful.** Ban in chrome: Decided, Semantic massing, pl sequence, gates process, archviz, SpatialStructure, CityJSON (link as “City map” / “Plan” instead).
3. **Build order, not metaphysics.** Stages mean “what appears next,” not a paper process.
4. **Address the reader.** “Click a building” not “Selected place object.”
5. **Verify** by looking at the rendered rail at desktop width — if a teammate cannot say what the page *is* in five seconds, rewrite.
